# Origins
Origins are selectable once you've created your character. They provide an initial goal for you to strive for and act as a jumping off point for roleplay. There is a small debuff to experience gain until you complete your origin quest. Completing your origin quest grants you a small buff.

---

### A Place to Call Home
I'm tired of drifting. Inns and camps were fine once, but now I want more than a bed for the night. I want a place that's mine. A real home, bought honestly, with walls to return to and a door I can lock behind me. A place to store my belongings, a place to call home.

Reward: Buy/Sell Prices -/+ 5%

### Ash and Ancestry
My family's amulet was lost generations ago, stolen during the age of the Dragon Cult and buried with one of its servants. Old records and stories point toward the Nordic ruins scattered throughout <Alias=Hold>. I must search these ancient places and recover what was taken from my bloodline.

Reward: Ancestral Amulet (Fortify Health Rank 1: +50 Health)

### Ashes of the Dragon Cult
Generations ago, my ancestors were enslaved under the cruel rule of a Dragon Priest. His temples were built with the blood and suffering of my family. Now whispers speak of the dead walking again, and the priest may have returned. I must ensure that justice is served once and for all.

Reward: +10% Damage to Draugr

### Beneath the Brass
The Dwemer have always fascinated me. One man in particular might have held the key to all their secrets: Dwemer Engineer Dumarac. My research points he may have lived in Skyrim, in what today is known as <Alias=Hold>. I will search all their ruins in the hold until I uncover his notes.

Reward: + 10% Damage to Dwemer Automatons

### Blind Justice
A guard has abused the authority of the uniform for personal gain, intimidating others and escaping all accountability. With no other path to justice, I have decided to take matters into my own hands. I will kill the guard myself.

Reward: +10% Damage to Guards

### Blood of the Reach
The Forsworn burned my home and butchered those who couldn't run. I survived, but I never forgot the man who led the raid. Korrak. I will find him, and I will kill him.

Reward: +10% Damage to Forsworn

### Blue Sapphire
My father was executed for a crime he didn't commit, framed to hide the actions of another. Mother spent her life searching for answers, and in the end, she found them. The man responsible escaped justice and fled to Skyrim, marked by a gold ring set with a blue sapphire. I will find him, and see justice done.

Reward: Ring of the Blue Sapphire (+11% Magic Resist)

### Chaos Incarnate
We pretend to be civilized, but beneath it all we are still animals, ruled by fear and instinct. I refuse to play along anymore. I will push the world to its limit until the truth shows. Let people whisper my name. Let them come for me if they dare. I will teach them what chaos truly looks like. Skyrim will fear me.

Reward: +10% Damage to Guards

### Chasing the Rush
I live for the fall. For the silence before the leap and the rush that follows. Skyrim is filled with cliffs, bridges, and hidden ledges that beg to be tested, and I intend to try them all. I will find each one and jump without hesitation.

Reward: +10 Stamina

### Dark Trail
My friend Elara has been exploring Skyrim's hidden caves. Her last letter suggests she was exploring a cave with glowing mushrooms. She has not been heard of from since. I must explore the cave and uncover what happened to her.

Reward: +10% Damage to Falmer

### Duty and Honor
I've always admired thanes. The way their presence commands respect, not through fear, but through trust. To be sworn to a hold, to be counted on as its protector... That's the kind of purpose I long for. Duty, honor, and the weight of responsibility. I will earn that recognition.

Reward: Buy/Sell Prices -/+ 5%

### Golden Touch
I have always admired those who command wealth. While others chase glory on the battlefield, I chase something far more tangible: Gold. I will not stop until I have 10,000 Septims, enough to have achieved true mastery over my own fate.

Reward: Buy/Sell Prices -/+ 5%

### Hunter Becomes Hunted
They accused me of being a beast and tried to put me down like an animal. I escaped, but the Silver Hand never stopped hunting me. I am done running. I'm coming for their leader.

Reward: +10% Damage to Silver Hand

### Knight on the Road
Years ago, a man saved my life on the road, but died because of it. He carried a journal with him that mentioned a daughter who lives in Skyrim. I intend to find her.

Reward: Halmar's Ring (Fortify Health Rank 1: +30 Health)

### Left For Dead
Bandits slaughtered my parents on the road, and left me for dead. One name stayed with me through the pain and the darkness: Thorolf. I won't let him escape justice.

Reward: +10% Damage to Bandits

### Marked by the Moon
My friend Einar Fenrisson has entrusted me with a terrible secret. He has become a werewolf, and with every moon, the beast within him grows stronger. He is no longer in control, and wants me to put an end to his suffering.

Reward: +10% Damage to Werewolves

### No Questions Asked
A shadowy figure contracted me to kill a target. No justification was given, only a promise of gold taken from the corpse. I was told not to ask any questions, only to carry out the deed and take what I am owed.

Reward: 3000 Gold
### Raise those Fists
I've never been able to walk away from a bar fight. Fists speak clearer to me than words, and Skyrim is said to be a land where strength is tested honestly, over ale and bruises. If this land truly breeds the toughest brawlers, then I'll find them, one by one, and prove I belong among the best.

Reward: +5 Unarmed Damage

### Sight to See
I have heard much of the Shrine of Azura, high in the mountains near Winterhold. I wish to see it for myself and to follow the call of adventure that stirs within me.

Reward: +5 Stamina

### Sightseeing
The road calls for me. I want to see all of Skyrim... every city, every hold. Civil unrest and war be damned, there are stories to uncover, mysteries to witness, and sights to behold. Curiosity will guide me and I will visit every single hold capital.

Reward: +5 Stamina

### The Best of Intentions
I received a letter from my friend Aurelian. He mentioned a group of renegade mages he intended to confront, and I can't shake the feeling that something might go wrong. I must find them. I need to make sure he is safe.

Reward: +10% Damage to Necromancers and Warlocks

### The Final Promise
My mother passed away clutching the memory of a pendant stolen long ago. I promised her I would recover the amulet that had been in our family for generations. Though she is gone, the promise I made to her remains.

Reward: Generational Amulet (Fortify Health Rank 1: +50 Health)

### The Great Hunt
Legends speak of a sabre cat larger than any hunter has seen, faster than any arrow can follow, and deadly enough to kill even the most skilled. Many have sought it and failed. I will not. I will track this beast, face it, and honor my father's legacy with every step of the hunt.

Reward: +10% Damage to Beasts

### The Last Lesson
My mentor's pursuit of knowledge has led him stray. Rumors speak of necromancy, of bargains that cost more than they give. I don't know what remains of the man I once knew, but I can't turn away.

Reward: +10% Damage to Necromancers and Warlocks

### The Last Vigil
Cassian Volker, a Vigilant of Stendarr and a trusted friend, sent me a letter detailing his investigation into vampire activity. I have not heard from him since. I should find the vampire den and see what became of him.

Reward: +10% Damage to Vampires

### The Path of Freedom
My father died in the Great War, and all I have left of him is his ring. And for what? So the Empire can bow to the Dominion and dictate our gods? Skyrim deserves to stand on its own, just like Hammerfell has done. I will join the Stormcloaks.

Reward: Father's Ring (Fortify Health Rank 1: +30 Health)

### The Path of Love
I wear the amulet of Mara and dream of a life filled with love. Marriage, a home, perhaps children... The things I've always wanted. Skyrim holds endless possibilities, and I will keep my heart open, trusting that love awaits.

Reward: Amulet of Mara

### The Path of Shadows
I was given a ring by a Dark Brotherhood assassin after witnessing her work. Since then, I have followed every whisper and rumor of the Brotherhood. All signs point toward Windhelm. I feel the pull of the shadows, and I know this is the path I am meant to walk.

Reward: Ring of Shadows (Fortify Sneak: +15%)

### The Path of the Bard
My grandmother was a bard. Before she passed, she gave me her ring, and I promised I would follow in her footsteps. I will travel to Solitude and join the College of Bards to honor her memory. As long as I carry this ring, I know she is with me.

Reward: Ring of the Bard (Fortify Speech: +10%)

### The Path of the Mage
I have made up my mind. I will join the College of Winterhold to study magic and honor my father's memory. As long as I carry his ring, a part of him walks beside me. This is my path now, and I will follow it.

Reward: Father's Ring (Fortify Magicka Rank 1: +30 Magicka)

### The Path of the Soldier
The Dominion grows stronger while Skyrim tears itself apart. My father died in the Great War, and I will not let his sacrifice be in vain. With his ring in hand, I will join the Legion and fight to keep the Empire standing.

Reward: Father's Ring (Fortify Health Rank 1: +30 Health)

### The Path of the Thief
I was never meant for an honest life. Theft comes naturally to me, and the thrill matters more than the coin. Petty jobs no longer satisfy me. I have heard whispers of the Thieves Guild in Riften, and I intend to find out if they would let me join their ranks.

Reward: Ring of Shadows (Fortify Sneak: +15%)

### The Path of the Warrior
I have decided to follow in my mother's steps as a warrior. I will join the Companions in Whiterun, to learn their ways and honor her memory. As long as I carry her ring, a part of her walks with me. This is my path now.

Reward: Mother's Ring (Fortify Stamina Rank 1: +30 Stamina)

### The Price of Power
Selvara was always ambitious and driven. She sought more than the life she was given, but I never imagined she would give herself entirely to her greed. They say she's now a Hagraven of unusual cruelty, hiding among the Forsworn. I will find her and end the monster she has become.

Reward: +10% Damage to Hagravens

### The Standing Stones
The Standing Stones call to me. Thirteen monuments, scattered across Skyrim, each said to hold ancient power and purpose. I have studied their legends long enough. I must find them and see them for myself.

Reward: +10 Stamina

### The Quiet Above
I have always found peace in high places. Skyrim is a land of towering mountains, and I feel drawn to them, not for glory, but for the silence they offer. I will climb the highest mountains in all of Skyrim.

Reward: +10 Stamina

### Top of the World
I am setting out for the Throat of the World. To see the monastery of the Greybeards, to climb the seven thousand steps, and to stand at the highest place in all of Tamriel. Whether I am welcomed or turned away, it does not matter. Adventure calls, and I am ready.

Reward: +5 Stamina
### Trader's Folly
In a moment of weakness, I sold a family heirloom that had been passed down in my family for generations. I tracked down the trader, but the item had been sold to another merchant somewhere in Skyrim. I will search every market and every shop until the candlestick is back in my hands.

Reward: Buy/Sell Prices -/+ 5%

### Vampire Crusade
I was young, but I still remember the sound of her corpse hitting the floor. I saw how the vampire killed my mother right in front of me and I did nothing, but hide in fear. I am now done hiding. I hear he has resurfaced and I am coming for him.

Reward: +10% Damage to Vampires

### Words Left Unspoken
An old man gave his life trying to protect mine. In his journal, he wrote of a son he disowned for becoming a bard instead of a soldier. He meant to search all of Skyrim to beg for his forgiveness. Now that duty falls to me.

Reward: Ring of the Bard (Fortify Speech: +10%)
